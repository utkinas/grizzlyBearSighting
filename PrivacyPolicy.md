# Privacy Policy for BearAware (Bear Sighting Recorder)

**Last Updated: September 22, 2026**

Thank you for using the BearAware Sighting Recorder app ("the App"). This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve the service:

**1. Information Collected Automatically:**

- **Geolocation Data:** When you choose to Record Sighting at current location, we collect the precise latitude and longitude of your device at that moment. We do not track your location in the background or at any other time.
- **Date and Time:** We automatically record the date and time when a sighting is submitted.
- **Anonymous User Identifier:** To enable community features and secure the database, the App uses Firebase Authentication to assign you a unique, anonymous identifier (userId). This ID is not linked to your personal identity, name, or email address.

**2. Information You Provide:**

- When you choose Record Sighting at current location, you will provide a bear type and an optional comment.
- If you choose Record a past sighting instead, you will provide date/time, bear type, location of sighting, and an optional comment.

**3. Content Moderation Data:**

When you use the report or block features to flag inappropriate content or abusive users, we collect: the ID of the sighting or user you flagged, your anonymous user ID, the reason you selected from a predefined list, and a timestamp. Reports include a copy of the reported content (comment text, bear type, and sighting date) so we can review it even if the original sighting is later modified. This data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore and is only visible to the app administrator for moderation purposes.

## How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the core functionality of the App:

- To display the location, date, time, and your comment for a bear sighting on a shared community map and list.
- To allow all users of the App to view this community-sourced data.
- To operate content moderation: reports allow the administrator to identify and remove objectionable content; blocks allow users to hide content from a specific contributor in their own view.
- The anonymous userId is used to associate you with your own sightings. This allows you to delete your own entries and helps us secure the database. This userId is never displayed to other users.

## Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data (sighting locations, dates, comments, and moderation records) solely to operate the community bear-sighting feature, respond to safety-related reports, and comply with legal obligations. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

## How We Share Your Information

The purpose of this app is to share sighting information with the community. Therefore, the following data is publicly shared and visible to all other users of the App:

- The latitude and longitude of a sighting.
- The date and time of a sighting.
- The comment you provided with a sighting.

**User reports and block lists are NOT shared publicly.** Reports are visible only to the app administrator for moderation. Each user's block list is private to that user and is never shown to anyone else, including the blocked user.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties for marketing, advertising, or other purposes.

## Community Standards and Terms of Use

BearAware has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. By using the app, you agree not to submit content that is offensive, harmful, discriminatory, or misleading. The developer reserves the right to remove any content and eject any user who violates these standards, without notice. Reported content is removed from the reporter's view immediately, and we act on reports within 24 hours.

## Community Moderation

BearAware provides in-app tools for users to report inappropriate content and block abusive users:

- **Report** — Any sighting on the community map can be flagged as inappropriate. Reports are reviewed by the developer within 24 hours, and offending content is removed and offending users may be ejected.
- **Block** — Any user can be blocked. All sightings from a blocked user are hidden from the blocker's community map view.

Content submitted through the comment field also passes through an automated filter before it is saved. Comments containing profanity, links, contact information, or excessive shouting are not accepted and must be revised before submission.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Report any sighting that appears to contain inappropriate content.
- Block any user whose content you find objectionable.
- Delete any sighting you have personally contributed.
- Contact us at igrizzlybear.info@gmail.com to request deletion of all your data.

## Your Data Rights (EU / California Residents)

If you are a resident of the European Union or California, you have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA, including the right to access, correct, delete, and receive a copy of your data, and to know what data is being collected and how it is used. To exercise these rights, contact us at igrizzlybear.info@gmail.com.

## Data Security

Your data is stored on Google's Firebase Firestore service, which uses industry-standard security measures to protect data. We have implemented security rules to prevent unauthorized access and to ensure that users can only delete their own contributions.

## Data Retention

Sighting data is stored in our database indefinitely to maintain a historical record for the community. You have the right to delete any sighting that you have personally contributed at any time, which will permanently remove it from our database. You may also request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us.

## Age Requirement

BearAware is rated 18+ in the App Store because it contains user-generated content. This application is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 18.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
