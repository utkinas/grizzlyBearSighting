# Privacy Policy for BearAware (Bear Sighting Recorder)

**Last Updated:** September 15, 2025

Thank you for using the BearAware app, also known as Bear Sighting Recorder ("the App"). This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use our application. This policy applies to both the iOS and Android versions of the App.

## Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve the service:

### 1. Information Collected Automatically

- **Geolocation Data:** When you choose to *Record Sighting at current location*, we collect the precise latitude and longitude of your device at that moment. We do not track your location in the background or at any other time.
- **Date and Time:** We automatically record the date and time when a sighting is submitted.
- **Anonymous User Identifier:** To enable community features and secure the database, the App uses Firebase Authentication to assign you a unique, anonymous identifier (`userId`). This ID is not linked to your personal identity, name, or email address.

### 2. Information You Provide

- When you choose *Record Sighting at current location*, you will need to provide **Bear type** and a **comment** (optional).
- If you choose *Record a past sighting* instead, you will need to provide **Date/Time**, **Bear type**, **location of sighting**, and a **comment** (optional).

## How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the core functionality of the App:

- To display the location, date, time, and your comment for a bear sighting on a shared community map and list.
- To allow all users of the App to view this community-sourced data.
- The anonymous `userId` is used to associate you with your own sightings. This allows you to delete your own entries and helps us secure the database. This `userId` is never displayed to other users.

## How We Share Your Information

The purpose of this app is to share information with the community. Therefore, the following data is publicly shared and visible to all other users of the App:

- The latitude and longitude of a sighting.
- The date and time of a sighting.
- The comment you provided with a sighting.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties for marketing, advertising, or other purposes.

## Data Security

Your data is stored on Google's Firebase Firestore service, which uses industry-standard security measures to protect data. We have implemented security rules to prevent unauthorized access and to ensure that users can only delete their own contributions.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Sighting data is stored in our database indefinitely to maintain a historical record for the community. You have the right to delete any sighting that you have personally contributed at any time, which will permanently remove it from our database. You may also request complete deletion of all your data by contacting us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
